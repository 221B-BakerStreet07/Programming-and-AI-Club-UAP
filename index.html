About Us

Our Mission: To inspire and educate students in the field of robotics through hands-on projects, workshops, and competitions.

We welcome members from all backgrounds — whether you're a beginner or an expert, there's a place for you here. Our club organizes regular meetups, guest lectures, and collaborative projects.

Focus Areas: Industrial automation, AI-powered robotics, drone technology, and human-robot interaction.
